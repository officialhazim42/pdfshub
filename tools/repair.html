Attempt to fix corrupted or malformed PDFs by rebuilding their structure.

Drop a PDF or click to upload

About Repair PDF

Repair PDF uses lenient pdf-lib loading and reserialization to rebuild a document that can still be parsed. It cannot restore missing data.

How to Use Repair PDF Online

  1. Upload the damaged PDF.
  2. Select Repair PDF to start lenient parsing.
  3. Wait while the document is reserialized.
  4. Open the repaired download and compare its pages with the original.

100% Client-Side Privacy

Files process in local browser memory via WebAssembly. Your documents are never uploaded to remote servers.

Supported Input

One damaged or malformed PDF

Output Result

A reserialized PDF when recovery succeeds

Practical Pro-Tip

Keep the damaged source and make a copy before experimenting.
