Capture camera images or upload photos and assemble them into a PDF.

Tool Overview & Requirements

Scan to PDF uses the camera when permitted or accepts uploaded images, optionally applies grayscale or high contrast, and embeds each image on a page.

Input Camera captures or JPG/PNG/WebP images
Output A multi-page scanned PDF
Requirements Camera mode requires browser permission and a secure origin. Upload mode accepts image files. The page can work with either source.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Follow these essential instructions to achieve optimal results when using Scan to PDF:

  1. Choose Start Camera and grant permission, or upload document images.
  2. Capture or add all pages and remove any incorrect image from the grid.
  3. Choose Fit image, A4, or Letter and an enhancement.
  4. Select Create PDF and review every scanned page.

Tips & Best Practices

  • Use even lighting and keep the camera parallel to the page.
  • Preview enhancement choices so small text remains readable.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Leaving the camera stream running after finishing.
  • Adding pages in the wrong order.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

If camera access is denied, use image upload or change the browser permission.

If the output is rotated, retake the image or rotate it before scanning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use this without a camera?

Yes, upload JPG, PNG, or WebP images.

Does it perform OCR?

No, use OCR PDF for text recognition.
