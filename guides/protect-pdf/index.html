Protect PDF Guide & Walkthrough

Add password encryption and permission settings to a PDF.

Open This Tool All Guides

Tool Overview & Requirements

Protect PDF uses pdf-lib password options to create an encrypted copy with a chosen password and permissions.

Input One PDF and a password
Output An encrypted PDF
Requirements One readable PDF is required. The password is entered locally and is not recoverable by PDFHub.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Follow these essential instructions to achieve optimal results when using Protect PDF:

  1. Upload a PDF.
  2. Enter a password twice and choose the available permission preset.
  3. Select Protect PDF.
  4. Open the downloaded file and test the password before deleting the source.

Step 1 of 7 Upload Drop Zone (#drop)

Open Protect PDF

What to do:

Open Protect PDF to encrypt your documents with AES encryption and strong passwords.

What you will see:

The upload drop area accepts any PDF document requiring confidentiality protection.

Next:

Click to select or drag your PDF document into the workspace.

Tip:

Protecting documents prevents unauthorized viewing, printing, and copying.

Step 2 of 7 File Browser (#fin)

Upload Document to Protect

What to do:

Select your confidential PDF file from your device.

What you will see:

The document loads and the password configuration controls appear.

Next:

Set a strong password to guard the document.

Tip:

Ensure you store the password securely, as encrypted files cannot be opened without it.

Step 3 of 7 Password Field (#pw)

Enter Strong Password

What to do:

Type a strong, secret password into the first password input.

What you will see:

The field masks characters for privacy and security.

Next:

Use a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.

Tip:

A passphrase with 10 or more characters provides high security against brute-force attacks.

Step 4 of 7 Confirm Password Field (#pw2)

Confirm Password Verification

What to do:

Re-type the exact same password into the confirmation field.

What you will see:

The verification field confirms that both password inputs match.

Next:

Proceed to adjust permission restrictions.

Tip:

If inputs do not match, the tool will alert you before encrypting.

Step 5 of 7 Permissions Selector (#perms)

Configure Permissions

What to do:

Choose permissions restrictions (e.g. Allow printing, Allow copying text, or Full lockdown).

What you will see:

Permission flags restrict what unauthorized viewers can do even after viewing.

Next:

Select "Require password to open" for complete confidentiality.

Tip:

Disabling printing prevents unauthorized physical paper distribution.

5 / 7
Step 6 of 7 Protect Action Button (#goBtn)

Click Protect PDF

What to do:

Click the primary "Protect PDF" button.

What you will see:

The client-side crypto engine encrypts the entire document stream using AES-256 cipher.

Next:

Wait for the encryption process to finish.

Tip:

Encryption happens 100% locally; neither your file nor your password ever leaves your machine.

Step 7 of 7 Download Dialog (#dlModal .btn-success)

Download Password-Protected PDF

What to do:

Click "Download" to save your encrypted PDF document.

What you will see:

The download modal confirms your file is encrypted with AES-256.

Next:

Save the protected document to your files.

Tip:

Anyone opening this document will now be prompted for the password before pages can render.

Tips & Best Practices

  • Use a unique, long password and store it securely.
  • Share the password through a separate channel from the document.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Forgetting the password.
  • Assuming viewer permissions are enforced equally by every PDF reader.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

If passwords do not match, re-enter both fields.

If encryption fails, retry with a readable PDF and a strong browser session.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can PDFHub recover a forgotten password?

No.

Does this upload the password?

The operation is performed in the browser.
