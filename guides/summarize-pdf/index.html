Tool Overview & Requirements

AI Summarizer extracts up to the configured page limit and builds a summary request for the selected style. Its final result depends on the configured external AI service and network availability.

Input A text-based PDF
Output Summary text and TXT download
Requirements Text-based PDFs work best. An external AI service may be required; do not use this tool for confidential text unless you understand that service’s terms.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Follow these essential instructions to achieve optimal results when using AI Summarizer:

  1. Upload a PDF and choose a summary style.
  2. Set the maximum number of pages to process.
  3. Select Summarize PDF and wait for extraction and the service response.
  4. Review the summary against the original, then copy or download it.

Tips & Best Practices

  • Verify important claims against the source document.
  • Use concise style for quick orientation and detailed style for a structured review.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Treating an AI summary as a substitute for the original.
  • Sending sensitive documents without checking the service behavior.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

If the request fails, check connectivity and retry with fewer pages.

If extraction is empty, run OCR first for a scanned PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the summary guaranteed accurate?

No; AI output can omit or misstate details.

Does this work fully offline?

The configured AI request may require network access.
