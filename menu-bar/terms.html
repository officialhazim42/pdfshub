04

Processing model and browser storage

PDFHub is designed as a browser-first application. For standard tools (including merge, split, compress, edit, convert, watermark, and sign), processing operations occur locally within your browser's execution memory using WebAssembly and JavaScript libraries. Original files are not transmitted to any PDFHub backend server.

For your convenience, PDFHub stores minimal local state in your browser's localStorage (such as your dark/light theme setting, pinned favorite tools, and recent tool shortcuts). This data resides exclusively on your local device. On public or shared computers, you should use the Manage Local Data control in the Privacy Center to clear this information before ending your session.
