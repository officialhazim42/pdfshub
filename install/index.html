Android Installation (Chrome & Edge)

Installs directly from your mobile browser with home screen & app drawer icons.

  1. 1
    Open PDFHub in Google Chrome or Edge

    Ensure you are visiting pdfshub.smartstores.in in standard browsing mode.

  2. 2
    Tap the Install Banner or Browser Menu

    If an "Add PDFHub to Home screen" banner appears at the bottom, tap it. Otherwise, tap the ⋮ (three dots) menu in the top-right corner of Chrome.

  3. 3
    Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen"

    In the dropdown menu, locate and tap Install app (or Add to Home screen in Samsung Internet).

  4. 4
    Confirm Installation

    Tap Install in the dialog. The PDFHub icon will appear on your Home Screen and in your application drawer, launching without browser URL bars.

Samsung Internet or Firefox users: Tap the hamburger menu ☰ or three dots ⋮ and choose Add page to > Home screen.

iPhone & iPad Installation (Apple Safari)

Apple iOS requires Safari's native "Add to Home Screen" feature for PWA installation.

  1. 1
    Open in Apple Safari

    If you are currently reading this in Chrome, Firefox, or an in-app browser on iOS, tap Copy Web Address above, open Safari, and paste the URL.

  2. 2
    Tap the Share Button

    Tap the Share icon located in the bottom toolbar on iPhone, or in the top navigation bar on iPad.

  3. 3
    Choose "Add to Home Screen"

    Scroll down the share sheet options and tap Add to Home Screen .

  4. 4
    Confirm and Tap "Add"

    Confirm the app name is "PDFHub" and tap Add in the top-right corner. The PDFHub icon is now pinned to your Home Screen with instant full-screen execution.

iPadOS Note: iPadOS supports full split-screen multitasking with installed PWAs, allowing you to drag documents directly into PDFHub alongside Files or Notes!

Windows 11 / 10 Installation (Edge & Chrome)

Run PDFHub as a standalone desktop application with taskbar pinning and window framing.

  1. 1
    In Microsoft Edge:

    Click the App Available icon on the right side of the URL address bar, or click Settings (...) > Apps > Install PDFHub.

  2. 2
    In Google Chrome:

    Click the Install icon in the address bar, or click ⋮ > Cast, save, and share > Install PDFHub.

  3. 3
    Pin to Taskbar & Start Menu

    When the installation prompt appears, check "Pin to taskbar" and "Pin to Start" for convenient single-click launching.

Standalone Window: PDFHub will launch in its own clean window without tabs or search bars, maintaining complete keyboard shortcuts and private local file access.

macOS Installation (Safari 17+ or Chrome)

Add PDFHub directly to your Mac Dock or Applications folder.

  1. 1
    In Safari (macOS Sonoma 14+):

    In the top menu bar, click File > Add to Dock.... Confirm the title "PDFHub" and click Add.

  2. 2
    In Chrome or Edge on Mac:

    Click the install icon in the URL bar or navigate to Menu > Install PDFHub to save it directly into your Mac's Applications folder.

Linux Desktop Installation

Integrates seamlessly with GNOME, KDE, and modern desktop application launchers.

  1. 1
    In Chromium or Google Chrome:

    Click the install icon in the Omnibox or choose Menu > Install PDFHub.

  2. 2
    Desktop Launcher (.desktop file):

    The system automatically registers a desktop entry so you can launch PDFHub through your application drawer or Super/Windows key search.

Troubleshooting & FAQs

Why don't I see an install button?

Browsers hide the native install prompt if the app is already installed, if you are browsing in Private/Incognito mode, or if you are using an in-app browser (such as inside Twitter/Instagram). Use your browser's standard menu instead.

Does PDFHub work offline?

Yes, for core client-side PDF operations! Previously loaded tools (like Merge, Split, Compress, Rotate, and Crop) work locally in your browser without requiring an active internet connection.

Are my PDF documents private?

100% private. Standard operations process your files in client-side WebAssembly and JavaScript directly on your device. Your documents are never uploaded to a remote server.

How do I uninstall or reinstall?

In Chrome/Edge desktop: Click the ⋮ menu in the app title bar and select Uninstall PDFHub. On Android or iOS: Long-press the PDFHub home icon and tap Remove or Uninstall.
